report on the screen, twelve lines to a page (docs/toy-wire-stream-test-79.md,

3000 x 4000 pixels each, and sent them as two archives. The photographs are
the owner's and are not in this repository; what identifies them is below.

The file names carry the time of each photograph (America/Chicago): Evolution's
pages at 10:42:47 to 10:43:01, Evolution 2's at 10:47:24 to 10:47:36. The
pages go round; Evolution 2's were photographed from the second on.

Every page shows, above the report's lines, the worker's own three lines
(HYBRID, BUILD 9F183A7F72F5, NATIVE), the heading REPORT NOT SAVED / FILM
EVERY PAGE and, below them, START / REBOOT TO K-UI CD. Pages 1 to 3 hold
twelve lines of the report each, page 4 the last six: 42 lines a report.

console-report-evolution.txt and console-report-evolution-2.txt are those 42
lines in the report's order, as the screen shows them. (The screen's letters
are capitals only: a saved report has the build as 9f183a7f72f5.)

How they were read
  1. By the author, from crops of the photographs at their full resolution.
  2. A second time by two readers that were given the photographs and
     nothing of the first reading, one reader a game. Each compared every
     8 and B, 0 and D, 5 and 6, 3 and 9, C and E, E and F against glyphs of
     the same photograph whose identity the words give (BUILD, HYBRID, the
     labels, 781250). Neither listed a character in doubt.
  3. The two readings were compared by a program: equal in every character,
     42 lines of 42 for each game.

Checks the reports make of themselves
  PIPE's CALLS and IRQ are the row V's GAME and SCI: 909C and 49C7 on pages
  1 and 3 of Evolution, 2359 and 1E67 on pages 1 and 3 of Evolution 2.
  X's OW, the windows opened, is S's first value, the streams closed with
  their windows: 4B and 4B, 3CC and 3CC.
  K's LIGHT and EDC, less W FILL's BAD, are the sectors verified into
  windows (AHEAD's EDC), but for sectors refused once whole: Evolution 2
  12,707 + 2,639 - 4 = 15,342 = 3BEE exactly; Evolution 32,622 + 3,946 - 12
  = 36,556 against 8EC8 = 36,552, with 27 sectors refused (REF 1B).
  Evolution 2's bytes to a block, 2526B44 / 11BEE = 535.9, are those of the
  Toy Commander runs (535.5 to 536.2). Evolution's are 548.0: twelve bytes
  more of the card's own pauses to a block. The digit this rests on, the 8 of
  581095C, was looked at again by the author for that reason, cell by cell
  against the 6 and the B of the same line: it has the stroke at the upper
  right that a 6 lacks, and the cut corners and waist at the left that a B
  does not have. Both readings have it as 8.

The launcher's preflight on original boot programs, with K-UI's memory limit
===========================================================================

Production code: native_cache_prepare_repeat (src/apps/games_native_cache.inc) of commit 53a33ec7
(run at 0c7a9db7 first; the three result tables are byte for byte the same),
through the repository's fixture I/O, on the 426 boot programs of the owner's CD-audio archives
(kept outside the repository; no game byte is recorded here). Module: the owner's New reader module of
26 November 2026 (Test 84's retail-native-dual.kui, build 4abc63b0cc58, payload CRC32 BAFAAF7C).
Both Background readers and both Virtual VMU settings: 1,704 launches a run.

The whole-program allocation is refused above a byte limit (the launcher's KUI_NATIVE_CACHE_BOOT_ALLOC
stand-in), as the console refuses it. 'whole-program answer' is the same launch without a limit
(docs/evidence/kui-launch-checks-d41a04f/corpus.txt). For every launch the CRC the launch would use
was compared with an independent CRC of the file; every piecewise read took one read per 16 sectors.
The independent probe includes src/core/vmu_native_discovery.c as it is and counts, with the
discovery's own table, loop bounds and sdk_at, the sites of each program.

Host decisions only. Nothing here ran on a console.

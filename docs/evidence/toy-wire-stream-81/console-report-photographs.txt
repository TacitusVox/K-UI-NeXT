The file names and the pictures' own records carry the time of each photograph
(America/Chicago): Evolution's pages at 15:36:50 to 15:36:57, Evolution 2's at
15:41:08 to 15:41:19. Evolution was run first.

Every page shows, above the report's lines, the worker's own three lines
(HYBRID, BUILD 684F485644E5, NATIVE), the heading REPORT NOT SAVED / FILM EVERY
PAGE and, below them, START / REBOOT TO K-UI CD. Each page holds twelve lines
of the report: 36 lines a report.

console-report-evolution.txt and console-report-evolution-2.txt are those 36
lines in the report's order, as the screen shows them. (The screen's letters
are capitals only: a saved report has the build as 684f485644e5.)

How they were read
  1. By the author, from crops of the photographs at their full resolution.
  2. Evolution 2's a second time, by a reader that was given the photographs
     and nothing of the first reading. It compared the look-alikes (0, 8, B,
     D) against glyphs of the same photograph whose identity the words give,
     and left no character in doubt. The two readings were compared by a
     program: equal in every character, 36 lines of 36.
  3. Evolution's pages have the author's reading alone. Their second reader
     did not finish (the session's usage limit). The photographs are sharp,
     and every check below holds for them; the values no check covers (the
     rows SD, VMAX, P, D, the three W rows and BIG) rest on the one reading.

Checks the reports make of themselves
  PIPE's CALLS and IRQ are the row V's GAME and SCI: 35B1 and 2FCB on pages 1
  and 2 of Evolution, 1DC4 and 180B on pages 1 and 2 of Evolution 2.
  X's OW, the windows opened, is S's first value, the streams ended with their
  windows: 1F and 1F, 16 and 16.
  K's LIGHT and EDC, less W FILL's BAD, are the sectors verified into windows
  (AHEAD's EDC) exactly, with no sector refused in either run: Evolution
  24,944 + 2,738 - 14 = 27,668 = 6C14; Evolution 2 11,888 + 1,375 - 4 =
  13,259 = 33CB.
  S's second and third values are what the other rows hold: Evolution E = REF
  0 + BAD E, D7 = LF D4 + AM 0 + BC 3; Evolution 2 4 = REF 0 + BAD 4, 7C =
  LF 7A + AM 0 + BC 2.
  The stream's bytes to a block: Evolution 42EAA0A / 1F253 = 550.0, Evolution 2
  206B187 / EF0A = 555.5.
  PT is 1FBD in both, which this build's switches give, and TOY is 0: a title
  that is not Toy Commander.

decode_report.py in this folder turns the lines into the figures the document
quotes (console-report-decoded.txt, with Test 80's and Test 79's reports
beside them). It is Test 79's decoder with the rows Tests 80 and 81 added.

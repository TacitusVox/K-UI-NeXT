Test 80 on the console, 16 November 2026: the report's pages as photographed

A launch from the Games menu brings no report file, so the worker shows the
report on the screen, twelve lines to a page (docs/toy-wire-stream-test-80.md,
The report). The owner photographed the three pages of the Evolution 2 run with
a telephone, 3000 x 4000 pixels each, and sent them as one archive. The
photographs are the owner's and are not in this repository; what identifies
them is below.

The file names and the pictures' own records carry the time of each photograph
(America/Chicago): 15:30:58 to 15:31:06. The pages go round; they were
photographed from the second on.

Every page shows, above the report's lines, the worker's own three lines
(HYBRID, BUILD E58DD2E6B641, NATIVE), the heading REPORT NOT SAVED / FILM EVERY
PAGE and, below them, START / REBOOT TO K-UI CD. Each page holds twelve lines
of the report: 36 lines.

console-report-evolution-2.txt is those 36 lines in the report's order, as the
screen shows them. (The screen's letters are capitals only: a saved report has
the build as e58dd2e6b641.)

How they were read
  1. By the author, from crops of the photographs at their full resolution.
  2. A second time by a reader that was given the photographs and nothing of
     the first reading. It measured the look-alikes (0, 8, B, D) against
     glyphs of the same photograph whose identity the words give, and against
     the program's own glyph table.
  3. The two readings were compared by a program: equal in every character,
     36 lines of 36.

The first page's photograph (20261116_153106156.jpg) is blurred: the holes of
0, 8, B and D are nearly closed in it. Both readers have its values the same.
What holds them beside the reading:
  PIPE's CALLS and IRQ are the row V's GAME and SCI on the second page, which
  is sharp: 1F5B and 20B2 on both.
  PT is FBD, which this build's switches give.
  The values that rest on the blurred page alone: STAY 42; AHEAD's HIT 51B,
  EDC 347A, STOP 0 and OS 3031; X's row 1F 0 0 0 0 1E; BIG's row 10 5B2 0 0.
  Of those, K's LIGHT and EDC less W FILL's BAD (2E84 + 5FD - 2 = 13,439) are
  five more than AHEAD's EDC (347A = 13,434), with 33 sectors refused once
  whole (REF 21): the same relation as in Test 79's Evolution report. OS over
  HIT is 9.4 sectors a request, as in Test 81's run of the same game (9.5).

Checks the report makes of itself
  S's second and third values, the streams ended for a sector that failed and
  for a reader that could not follow, are what the other rows hold: 23 = REF
  21 + BAD 2; 83 = LF 83 + AM 0 + BC 0.
  The stream's bytes to a block, 20A5809 / F246 = 551.9, are those of the
  other runs (535.9 to 555.5).
  X's OW, the windows opened, is 1F; S's first value, the streams ended with
  their window, is 1D. In Test 79's reports the two were equal. Here one
  window was still open when the report was asked for, and one was given up
  with no stream running behind it: with 35 streams ended for a failed sector
  that can happen, and Test 81's reports have the two equal again.

decode_report.py of docs/evidence/toy-wire-stream-81/ turns the lines into the
figures the document quotes (console-report-decoded.txt, with Test 79's report
of the same game beside it).

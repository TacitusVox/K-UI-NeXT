## FMV performance theory

- **Movie players use the audio as their clock.** When K-UI takes CPU time,
  or a read is slow, the picture falls behind while the audio stays correct.
  Armada VMU On proved this: a 14% hook made a slideshow, and under 1% fixed
  it.
- **Native CD-music games, Toy Commander most of all:**
  - The CD-music route has no background reader.
  - Toy's movie reads tiny pieces and waits for each one: 540 reads,
    about 38 ms each, first byte after about 11 ms. About 22 s of the intros
    are spent waiting, about 97% of it inside K-UI's reader.
  - The fix direction is sequential read-ahead on that route.
  - The deciding console test (not yet run): Toy with CD Music Off and
    Background On.
